## What's in the box

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="9 algo strategies" icon="rocket">
    LIMIT, TWAP, VWAP, POV, ICEBERG, SNIPER, ARRIVAL_PRICE, IS, and MOMENTUM — each an independent service on the Redpanda bus.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pre-trade risk engine" icon="warning">
    Six checks: fat-finger collar, duplicate detection, rate limit, self-cross, ADV, max open orders. Hard-fail when down — orders are blocked, not bypassed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Realistic desk segregation" icon="puzzle">
    18 personas across equity cash, equity derivatives, FI rates, FI credit, FX, and commodities. Cross-asset trading is impossible; high-touch vs low-touch enforced per panel.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fixed income & derivatives" icon="document">
    Bond pricing, yield curves, duration ladders, spread analysis, vol surfaces, Black–Scholes options, and scenario matrices.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Live P&L and positions" icon="bars">
    Real-time positions from fills with unrealised, realised, and total P&L. Risk Dashboard for firm-wide oversight; My Positions for the individual trader.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Session replay" icon="magnifier">
    Record and play back browser sessions with rrweb for training, audit, and post-incident review. Admin-controlled with timeline playback.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
