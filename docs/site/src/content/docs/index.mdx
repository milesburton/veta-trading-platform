## Capabilities

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Algorithmic execution" icon="bars">
    Nine execution strategies: LIMIT, TWAP, VWAP, POV, ICEBERG, SNIPER, ARRIVAL_PRICE, IS, and MOMENTUM. Each runs as an independent service on the Redpanda bus.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pre-trade risk controls" icon="warning">
    Six real-time checks: fat-finger collar, duplicate detection, rate limit, self-cross, ADV participation, and maximum open orders. The engine hard-fails closed; orders are blocked rather than bypassed when it is unavailable.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Desk segregation" icon="puzzle">
    Eighteen personas across equity cash, equity derivatives, FI rates, FI credit, FX, and commodities. Cross-asset trading is prevented, and high-touch versus low-touch workflows are enforced per panel.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fixed income and derivatives" icon="document">
    Bond pricing, yield curves, duration ladders, spread analysis, volatility surfaces, Black–Scholes options pricing, and scenario matrices.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Live P&L and positions" icon="bars">
    Positions are derived from fills in real time, with unrealised, realised, and total P&L. A Risk Dashboard provides firm-wide oversight; My Positions covers the individual trader.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Session replay" icon="magnifier">
    Browser sessions are recorded and replayed with rrweb for training, audit, and post-incident review, under administrator control with timeline playback.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
