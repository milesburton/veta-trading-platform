title: Route exposure
description: Which routes are reachable from the internet and which stay on the internal network, generated from the production compose file.
---

The platform exposes three services publicly through the edge proxy and the server's Traefik: the
frontend, the gateway (which also serves the `/health` router) and the FIX gateway. Every other
service is reachable only on the internal Docker network.

A router is public when its container sets `traefik.enable=true` and the router uses the
`websecure` entrypoint, the TLS entrypoint that the edge proxy forwards to. A container with
`traefik.enable=false` is never served by Traefik in production, so its router labels are inert.
Those services are reached only by the gateway over the internal Docker network.

Public does not mean unauthenticated. The gateway authenticates every route except the
allowlist below, and the proxy applies a minimum role per downstream service. The role sets are
read from the committed role matrix.

The unauthenticated gateway paths are `/health`, `/ready`, `/services/status`, `/platform-status`,
the WebSocket upgrades `/ws/gateway` and `/ws/market-sim`, the user-service paths under
`/oauth/`, `/auth/` and `/personas`, and `POST /events/batch` on the Kafka relay. A test
(`gateway-route-auth-inventory.unit.test.ts`) fails if any other route answers anonymously.

The tables below are generated by `route-exposure.unit.test.ts`, which fails when they drift from
`compose.prod.yml` or the role matrix. Regenerate them with `UPDATE_ROUTE_EXPOSURE=1`.
