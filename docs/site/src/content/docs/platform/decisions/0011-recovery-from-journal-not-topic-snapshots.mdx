---
title: "ADR 0011: Recover from the Postgres journal, not Redpanda topic snapshots"
description: "Records why the platform does not snapshot Redpanda topics and treats the Postgres journal and daily dump as the only recovery path for order history."
---

**Status:** Proposed

**Date:** 2026-11-01

## Context

Redpanda carries every order, fill, risk and market event. After a host loss, the question is
whether topic contents must be restored or whether they can be rebuilt.

- Topic retention is short. The cluster default is six hours and the market data topics keep one
  hour, set by the topic init job in `compose.yml`. A snapshot would hold at most that window.
- Consumers subscribe with `fromBeginning: false`. A restarted service reads new events only, so
  no service depends on historic topic content to start.
- The order history of record is `journal.events`, written from the same topics and kept
  indefinitely. Fills are also archived in `fix_archive.executions`.
- The [disaster recovery runbook](https://github.com/milesburton/veta-trading-platform/blob/main/docs/incidents/runbooks/disaster-recovery.md)
  already states that Redpanda is not restored and that topics are recreated empty.

## Decision

Do not snapshot Redpanda topics. The Postgres journal, restored from the daily verified dump, is
the recovery path for order history. In-flight events that were not yet journalled when the host
was lost are accepted as lost, which is covered by the recovery point objective of the dump.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| Periodic topic snapshots to object storage | Captures at most six hours of data, none of which services read on start. Adds a restore path that nothing exercises. |
| Tiered storage with long retention | Raises disk and operating cost to keep data already held in the journal. |
| Replay the journal into topics after a restore | No consumer needs it, and replaying orders risks re-submitting work. A read-only replay for analysis already exists in the replay service. |

## Consequences

- The recovery point for order data is the age of the newest Postgres dump, not the topic
  retention. Shortening it needs a better backup cadence, tracked in
  [#955](https://github.com/milesburton/veta-trading-platform/issues/955) and
  [#996](https://github.com/milesburton/veta-trading-platform/issues/996).
- Anything that is produced to a topic but never journalled has no recovery path. A new topic
  whose content must survive a host loss needs a journal consumer in the same change.
- The disaster recovery runbook links to this record in its Redpanda row.
